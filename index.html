My name is Yukun Huang (you can call me Charli). I am currently a PhD student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). Before starting my PhD, I received a B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Beijing Forestry University.

My research centers on Computer Graphics & 3D Vision:
  • Building robust methods for 3D reconstruction and streaming / online reconstruction.
  • Exploring rendering and practical 3D representations for dynamic, real-world scenes.
